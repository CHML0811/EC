# The 30-Day Plan

> Written against relative days, not calendar dates — it starts whenever you do.
> **Day 1 is the day the Etsy listing goes live**, not the day you start reading.

Goal: **first 10 paid orders and one validated title pattern.**

---

## The honest target

You asked what 30 days of AI ecommerce earns. Straight answer: **Etsy needs 30–90 days to
rank a listing**, and the new-listing boost lasts 14–21 days. So 30 days does not produce a
business. It produces the thing a business is built on.

| Outcome | Realistic by day 30 |
|---|---|
| Listings live | **8** |
| Orders | **5–20** |
| Revenue | **$180–650** |
| Profit | **$70–280** |
| What you actually gain | **Which pattern converts** — worth more than the revenue |

Anyone promising $10k in month one is selling a course. The compounding is real but it starts
in month 2–3, and only if the first 30 days generate clean data instead of noise.

**One accelerant worth using:** Etsy Ads at **$1–3/day**. It buys impressions immediately
while organic ranking builds, and at that budget the downside is capped at ~$60 for the month.
Turn it on at day 14, not day 1 — let the listings settle first.

---

## Week 1 · Days 1–7 — Foundation

**You (≈45 min, one time)**
1. Shopify → Settings → General → name **Deadpan Goods** · timezone **(GMT+8) Hong Kong**
2. **Printify** account → connect to Shopify → free
3. **Etsy** seller account → connect Printify → $0.20/listing
4. Activate **Shopify Payments + PayPal** (needs ID + bank)

**Me (done or in progress)**
- ✅ 8 certificate designs, print-ready 300dpi — `design/out/`
- ✅ 8 Etsy listings written: titles, 13 tags each, descriptions — `marketing/etsy-cohort-1.md`
- ✅ Policies, About, FAQ — `store/policies-and-pages.md`
- ✅ US shipping fixed · Halloween collection · bundle
- ⏳ Push the 8 certificates into Shopify as products

**Gate:** all 8 live on Etsy by **Aug 15**. Publish as one cohort, same day.

## Week 2 · Days 8–14 — Let it settle

**Rule of the week: do not touch the listings.** Every edit restarts Etsy's learning.

**Me:** Pinterest pin copy (5 per listing = 40 pins, free traffic, gift shoppers plan there) ·
draft Cohort 2 · review the numbers once a week (see `workflow/growth-loop.md`).

**You:** pin the Pinterest images (~20 min). Nothing else.

**Day 14 checkpoint:** any impressions at all? If a listing has **zero** by Aug 22, its tags
are wrong — that's the one exception to the no-touch rule. Turn on Etsy Ads at $2/day.

## Week 3 · Days 15–21 — Read the early signal

**Me:** analyse impressions and favourites by pattern (occasion / recipient / personalised) ·
build **Cohort 2 (8 more designs)** weighted toward whichever pattern is pulling · start the
**Christmas / Secret Santa** set, because that season is the real prize.

**You:** nothing.

**Day 21 checkpoint:** ≥100 visits across the cohort. Below that it's an SEO problem, not an
offer problem — I rewrite titles and tags for the laggards only.

## Week 4 · Days 22–30 — First judgement

**Day 30 is when Etsy starts ranking on real behaviour.** Now we read it properly.

| Check | Pass | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Visits | ≥100/listing | Rewrite title + tags |
| Conversion | ≥1.5% | Many favourites → price/photos. Few → SEO. |
| Orders | ≥5 total | Pattern is wrong; pivot Cohort 2 |
| Reviews | ≥4.8★ | Printer or product problem |

**Me:** publish Cohort 2 (8 listings) · full pattern report · Christmas cohort ready for
October · re-price apron to $45 and long-sleeve to $52.

## What happens after 30 days

| Month | Focus | Realistic |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Cohort 2 + 3, Christmas set built | $600–2,000 |
| **3** | **Christmas peak — the real one** | **$2,000–6,000** |
| 4 | Post-season: keep winners, kill the rest | $800–2,000 |

**Christmas is the actual goal.** Secret Santa and office gifting are peak season for exactly
what we're selling, and listings published in August will be fully ranked by then. The 30 days
is not the payoff — it's making sure we're ranked when the money shows up.

## Budget

| Item | 30 days |
|---|---|
| Etsy listing fees (8 × $0.20) | $1.60 |
| Printify | $0 |
| Shopify | already paid — **downgrade Advanced $399 → Basic $39** |
| Etsy Ads (from day 14, $2/day) | ~$32 |
| **Total new spend** | **~$34** |

**COGS only bills after a sale**, so there's no inventory risk. The one real money decision is
the Shopify plan: **Advanced is costing you $360/month for features you will not use for a
year.** Downgrading is the single highest-return action available this week.

---

## How this fails

| Risk | Guard |
|---|---|
| Panic-editing listings in week 2 | The no-touch rule. Etsy is learning; let it. |
| Quitting at week 3 with 2 sales | That's the normal curve. Month 3 is the payoff. |
| Adding products instead of fixing patterns | One cohort at a time. Read it before adding. |
| Personalisation typos → refunds | Review each custom name before it prints |
| Staying on the $399 plan | Downgrade this week |
