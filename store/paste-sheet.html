3 · The eight listings

Publish all eight the same day — they're a cohort, and they only teach you something read against each other. Then don't touch them for 30 days.

4 · Before you spend anything

  1. Place one real test order through checkout with your own card.
  2. Confirm it lands in Printify and produces a tracking number.
  3. Only then turn on Etsy Ads — $2–3/day, and not before day 14.
Then stop. The listings need 30 days to be judged by Etsy. Editing inside that window restarts the learning and you'll never know what worked.
