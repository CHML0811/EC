Nine steps to your first listing.

Every field is here in the order Etsy asks for it, with a copy button. Keep this open beside the Etsy tab. Progress is saved in this browser, so you can stop halfway and come back.

The first 48 hours

  • Don't touch the listing. Etsy gives new listings a boost for 14–21 days. Editing resets how it's being measured.
  • Watch impressions, not views. Etsy Stats → Search. No impressions means the title doesn't match how people search — that's the only thing worth changing early.
  • Expect nothing for two weeks. Ranking takes 30–90 days. Silence in week one is normal, not a verdict.
  • List it again for Christmas this week. Same zip, seasonal keywords, one new image — about an hour. Seasonal printables need 6–8 weeks to index and office parties are booked through November, so mid-October is the last responsible date, not the target. Copy is written in marketing/etsy-christmas-awards-kit.md.
  • Pin both listings the same day. Pinterest is the main free traffic source for printables after Etsy search. One vertical pin using the exact title keyword, then three variants. No ads until the listing converts.
