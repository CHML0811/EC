Three listings. Nine steps each.

Every field is here in the order Etsy asks for it, with a copy button. Keep this open beside the Etsy tab. Progress is saved per listing in this browser, so you can stop halfway and come back.

The first 48 hours

  • Don't touch the listing. Etsy gives new listings a boost for 14–21 days. Editing resets how it's being measured.
  • Watch impressions, not views. Etsy Stats → Search. No impressions means the title doesn't match how people search — that's the only thing worth changing early.
  • Expect nothing for two weeks. Ranking takes 30–90 days. Silence in week one is normal, not a verdict.
  • Pin every listing the day you publish it. Pinterest is the main free traffic source for printables after Etsy search. One vertical pin using the exact title keyword, then three variants. No ads until a listing converts.
  • The tags don't overlap between listings, and that's deliberate — two listings competing for the same primary keyword means Etsy buries one of them.
