## Refund Policy

**30 days. No interrogation.**

If it didn't land — wrong size, arrived damaged, or it simply wasn't as funny as you hoped —
email us within 30 days of delivery and we'll refund you. You don't need to explain the joke.

**How it works**
1. Email **support@deadpangoods.com** with your order number.
2. We reply within 1 business day.
3. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

**Returns.** For most items under $40 we won't ask you to ship it back — keep it, or pass it
on to someone who deserves it. For higher-value items we'll send a return label.

**What we can't refund.** Personalized items where the customisation is exactly what you
asked for, and items damaged by use rather than by shipping. Custom text with a typo you
supplied is, regrettably, a monument to your own choices.

**Damaged or wrong item.** Send a photo. We'll replace or refund immediately — you keep the
original. We're not going to argue about a $26 gag gift.

## Shipping Policy

**Processing.** Orders are processed within 1–2 business days.

**Delivery**

| Destination | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| United States | 5–10 business days | $5.95 · **free over $60** |
| Rest of world | 10–21 business days | $14.95 |

All orders are tracked. You'll get a tracking number by email when it ships.

**Seasonal cutoffs.** Gifts are time-sensitive and we'd rather over-warn you:
- **Halloween:** order by **October 10**
- **Christmas:** order by **December 1**

Past those dates we can't guarantee arrival, and a Halloween decoration in November is just
a decoration.

**Customs.** International orders may attract duties set by your country. Those aren't ours
to collect or refund.

**Lost in transit.** If tracking hasn't moved in 10 business days, email us and we'll replace
or refund it. Not your problem to chase.

## Privacy Policy

**What we collect.** Your name, email, shipping address, and payment details when you order.
Payment card details are processed by Shopify Payments and PayPal — we never see or store them.
We also collect basic analytics (pages viewed, device type) to understand what's working.

**Why.** To fulfil orders, provide support, and — if you opt in — send you email about new
products. Nothing else.

**Who we share it with.** Only the parties needed to get your order to you: Shopify (store and
checkout), our fulfillment partners and shipping carriers, and our email provider. **We do not
sell your personal information.**

**Marketing.** Opt-in only. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works immediately.

**Your rights.** Email **privacy@deadpangoods.com** to access, correct, export, or delete your
data. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're in California (CCPA) or the EU/UK (GDPR), you
have additional rights and we'll honor them.

**Cookies.** We use cookies for the cart, checkout, and analytics. You can block them in your
browser; the cart will stop working, which is a tradeoff you're entitled to make.

**Retention.** Order records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require, then deleted.

## Page: About

# We are very serious about stupid things.

Most gag gifts are badly made. Thin cotton, cheap plastic, a joke that dies the moment you
touch the thing. The laugh lasts four seconds and the item lasts about the same.

We thought that was a waste of a good joke.

So we make gag gifts properly. Real fabric weight. Real canvas. A trophy with an actual
weighted base. The comedy comes from treating something profoundly stupid with complete
sincerity — and that only works if the object itself is genuinely good.

**Everything here passes one test:** would you send it to someone you know? Not "would you
find it mildly amusing in a listing" — would you actually spend real money to make one
specific person laugh?

That's the whole business. We're not trying to be the cheapest. We're trying to be the thing
you send when you want to be funny and you want it to land.

**Rules of the house**
- The joke never gets explained. If we have to tell you it's funny, we've already lost.
- The product is real. The absurdity is the point; the quality isn't a joke.
- 30 days, no interrogation. If it didn't land, we'll refund it.
- We punch at situations, never at people.

Welcome. Send something regrettable to someone you love.

## Page: FAQ

**Is this a real store?**
Yes. Real products, real tracking, real refunds. The tone is deadpan; the operation isn't.

**How long does shipping take?**
5–10 business days in the US, 10–21 elsewhere. Tracked. **Halloween cutoff: October 10.**

**Is the packaging discreet?**
Yes. Plain outer packaging and no invoice inside, so you can send things directly to the
recipient. Nobody has to know it was you.

**Can I send it straight to them as a gift?**
Yes — just enter their address at checkout. Add a note in the order comments and we'll leave
the invoice out (we do that anyway).

**What if it doesn't fit?**
Email us. Apparel sizing runs true to a standard US fit; the size chart is on each product
page. Wrong size inside 30 days gets a refund or a swap.

**What if it arrives damaged?**
Send a photo and we'll replace or refund immediately. Keep the original.

**Are the costumes safe?**
They're novelty items, not safety gear. Not flame-retardant unless stated. Adult supervision
for children, and please don't drive in an inflatable suit.

**Do you take returns on personalized items?**
Only if we made the error. Custom text you supplied — including its typos — is yours.

**Do you ship internationally?**
Yes, $14.95. Duties are set by your country and aren't included.

**Can I sell these / order in bulk?**
Email **support@deadpangoods.com**. We do wholesale for events, offices, and parties.

## Ready-to-run: push all four policies

Schema-validated ✅ (requires `write_legal_policies`). Run via `graphql_mutation` when the
Shopify connector is up. `shopPolicyUpdate` creates the policy if it doesn't exist yet — the
store currently has only a default Privacy policy.

```graphql
mutation SetPolicies(
  $refund: ShopPolicyInput!
  $shipping: ShopPolicyInput!
  $privacy: ShopPolicyInput!
  $terms: ShopPolicyInput!
) {
  refund:   shopPolicyUpdate(shopPolicy: $refund)   { shopPolicy { id type url } userErrors { field message } }
  shipping: shopPolicyUpdate(shopPolicy: $shipping) { shopPolicy { id type url } userErrors { field message } }
  privacy:  shopPolicyUpdate(shopPolicy: $privacy)  { shopPolicy { id type url } userErrors { field message } }
  terms:    shopPolicyUpdate(shopPolicy: $terms)    { shopPolicy { id type url } userErrors { field message } }
}
```

Variables: `type` is one of `REFUND_POLICY` / `SHIPPING_POLICY` / `PRIVACY_POLICY` /
`TERMS_OF_SERVICE`; `body` is the HTML of each section above.

**Pages** (About, FAQ) use `pageCreate` — or paste them in at
**Online Store → Pages**, which takes about two minutes.
