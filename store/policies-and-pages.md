## Refund Policy

**Digital downloads.** Because a file can't be returned once it's been downloaded, digital
orders aren't refundable. That's the standard everywhere and we're not going to pretend
otherwise. But if a file won't open, won't print, or isn't what the listing said it was,
email us and we'll fix it or refund you the same day. We would rather lose the sale than
have you stuck with something broken.

**Printed certificates.** 30 days, no interrogation. If it arrived damaged, arrived wrong, or
simply wasn't as funny as you hoped, email us within 30 days of delivery and we'll refund
you. You don't need to explain the joke.

**How it works**
1. Email us with your order number.
2. We reply within 1 business day.
3. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

**Returns.** For printed items under $60 we won't ask you to ship anything back — keep it, or
give it to someone who has earned it. For higher-value orders we'll send a return label.

**Personalization.** A certificate printed with the name you typed is not returnable for
containing the name you typed. A typo you supplied is, regrettably, a monument to your own
choices. If *we* made the error, we'll reprint it free.

**Damaged or wrong item.** Send a photo. We'll replace or refund immediately and you keep the
original. We're not going to argue about a paper certificate.

## Shipping Policy

**Digital downloads are not shipped.** The download link is on your confirmation page and in
your confirmation email, available immediately after checkout. There is nothing to wait for
and no delivery to worry about — which matters if you're ordering the day before the party.

**Printed certificates**

**Processing.** Printed orders are produced and dispatched within 1–2 business days.

| Destination | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| United States | 5–10 business days | $5.95 · **free over $60** |
| Rest of world | 10–21 business days | $14.95 |

All printed orders are tracked. You'll get a tracking number by email when it ships.

**Seasonal cutoffs.** Office parties happen on a fixed date and we'd rather over-warn you:

- **Christmas printing:** order by **December 1**
- **Christmas digital:** no cutoff. It's an instant download.

Past that date we can't guarantee a printed order arrives in time. The digital kit always
will, which is the honest answer if you've left it late.

**Customs.** International orders may attract duties set by your country. Those aren't ours
to collect or refund.

**Lost in transit.** If tracking hasn't moved in 10 business days, email us and we'll reprint
or refund. Not your problem to chase.

## Privacy Policy

**What we collect.** Your name, email, billing and shipping address, and payment details when
you order. Payment card details are processed by Shopify Payments — we never see or store
them. We also collect basic analytics (pages viewed, device type) to understand what's working.

**What we don't collect.** The names you type into the Awards Maker never reach us. It runs
entirely in your browser, offline, and sends nothing anywhere. We could not tell you who won
Most Likely To Reply All at your office even if we wanted to.

**Why.** To fulfil orders, provide support, and — if you opt in — send you email about new
products. Nothing else.

**Who we share it with.** Only the parties needed to get your order to you: Shopify (store and
checkout), our print partner for physical orders, shipping carriers, and our email provider.
**We do not sell your personal information.**

**Marketing.** Opt-in only. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works immediately.

**Your rights.** Email us to access, correct, export, or delete your data. We'll respond
within 30 days. If you're in California (CCPA) or the EU/UK (GDPR), you have additional
rights and we'll honor them.

**Cookies.** We use cookies for the cart, checkout, and analytics. You can block them in your
browser; the cart will stop working, which is a tradeoff you're entitled to make.

**Retention.** Order records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require, then deleted.

## Page: About

# Recognition, finally, for the things that don't deserve any.

Every company hands out awards for revenue, for targets, for quarters exceeded. Nobody ever
gets one for what they are actually known for — the reply-all, the camera that is never on,
the meeting that could have been an email, the yoghurt in the fridge that has developed
opinions.

The Bureau of Minor Achievements exists to fix that oversight.

We are a fictional government agency with a real print budget. We issue properly typeset
certificates — guilloche border, embossed-look seal, serial number, an issuing officer's
signature — that happen to certify something completely absurd. The joke works precisely
because the paperwork refuses to admit there is one.

**Everything here passes one test:** would you actually stand up in a room and read it out?
Not "is this mildly amusing in a listing" — would you put your name to it, hand it to a
specific colleague, and have the room laugh with them rather than at them?

That's the whole business.

**Rules of the house**

- The joke never gets explained. If we have to tell you it's funny, we've already lost.
- The document is real. The absurdity is the point; the typesetting isn't a joke.
- We punch at situations, never at people. Nobody should dread receiving one of these.
- If a file doesn't work, we fix it the same day.

Issued under no authority whatsoever.

## Page: FAQ

**Is this a real store?**
Yes. Real files, real printing, real refunds. The tone is deadpan; the operation isn't.

**Is the Bureau a real agency?**
No. It is a fictional one. The certificates confer no qualification, licence, honor or status
of any kind, which is the entire joke.

**How do the digital downloads work?**
You get the files immediately after checkout — download link on the confirmation page and in
your email. Nothing ships, so there's no delivery to worry about even if the party is tomorrow.

**Do I need Canva or any software?**
No. The Awards Maker is a single file you open in your browser like a web page. Nothing to
install, no account, no subscription, and it works with the internet switched off. Every
certificate is also included as a ready-to-print image if you'd rather skip the maker.

**Can I print these at home?**
Yes. Any printer, plain paper or card. They're built at 300dpi, so they hold up at a print
shop too if you want something heavier.

**Can I reuse them next year?**
Yes. Buy once, print forever, for your own workplace or event. You can rewrite the wording to
suit your own office. You just can't resell the files or sell what you print.

**How long does printing take?**
Printed certificates: 5–10 business days in the US, 10–21 elsewhere, tracked.
**Christmas print cutoff: December 1.** Digital has no cutoff.

**What if it arrives damaged?**
Send a photo and we'll reprint or refund immediately. Keep the original.

**Are digital orders refundable?**
Not once downloaded — a file can't be returned. But if it won't open, won't print, or isn't
what the listing described, email us and we'll fix it or refund you the same day.

**Do you take returns on personalized prints?**
Only if we made the error. Custom text you supplied — including its typos — is yours.

**Do you ship internationally?**
Yes, $14.95. Duties are set by your country and aren't included.

**Can I order for a whole department, or in bulk?**
Yes — email us. We do bulk and wholesale for offices, schools, and events, and a department
order is a lot cheaper per head than it looks.

## Ready-to-run: push all four policies

Schema-validated ✅ (requires `write_legal_policies`). Run via `graphql_mutation` when the
Shopify connector is up. `shopPolicyUpdate` creates the policy if it doesn't exist yet.

```graphql
mutation SetPolicies(
  $refund: ShopPolicyInput!
  $shipping: ShopPolicyInput!
  $privacy: ShopPolicyInput!
  $terms: ShopPolicyInput!
) {
  refund:   shopPolicyUpdate(shopPolicy: $refund)   { shopPolicy { id type url } userErrors { field message } }
  shipping: shopPolicyUpdate(shopPolicy: $shipping) { shopPolicy { id type url } userErrors { field message } }
  privacy:  shopPolicyUpdate(shopPolicy: $privacy)  { shopPolicy { id type url } userErrors { field message } }
  terms:    shopPolicyUpdate(shopPolicy: $terms)    { shopPolicy { id type url } userErrors { field message } }
}
```

Variables: `type` is one of `REFUND_POLICY` / `SHIPPING_POLICY` / `PRIVACY_POLICY` /
`TERMS_OF_SERVICE`; `body` is the HTML of each section above.

**Pages** (About, FAQ) use `pageCreate` — or paste them in at
**Online Store → Pages**, which takes about two minutes.
