# Brand Bible — "Horizontal Club" (Sleepmaxxing / Rest-Identity Brand)

Working name: **Horizontal Club**. The name is swappable — everything below (positioning,
palette, products, prompts) still works if you change it. Alternates: **Somni** (quiet-luxury
boutique feel), **Rest Ethic** (anti-hustle manifesto feel), **Off-Duty** (identity feel).

> ⚠️ Before you commit the name: check trademark (USPTO Class 25 apparel), the `.com`, and
> the @handle on IG/TikTok. We avoid IP traps on the way *in*, not just on designs.

---

## 1. Strategy — why this beats a red-ocean price war

- **Category reframe:** we don't sell "sleep merch." We sell a **rest identity** — rest-wear
  and objects for people who treat rest as status, discipline, and self-respect.
- **The enemy:** hustle culture. "Team no sleep," burnout-as-a-badge, the grind.
- **The one-liner:** **Rest is a flex.**
- **The moat (not price):** identity + community + a consistent aesthetic world. A cheaper
  seller can copy a slogan tee; they cannot copy a tribe, a voice, and a look people want to
  belong to. We compete on *meaning*, not margin.
- **Premium on purpose:** higher price is *part of the flex*. Quality blanks + elevated
  minimalism signal "I can afford to rest." Discounting would break the identity.

---

## 2. The tribe (avatar)

- **Who:** 22–38, urban/suburban, wellness-adjacent, burnt out on hustle, into "soft life,"
  slow mornings, Sunday resets, matcha, cozy/quiet-luxury aesthetics, romanticizing rest.
- **Their language (use verbatim in copy):** sleepmaxxing, bed rotting, soft life, slow
  morning, "protecting my peace," "romanticize your rest," "canceled the plans," Sunday reset.
- **What they buy it to signal:** *"I have my life together enough to rest — and I'm proud
  of it."* Rest as luxury, not laziness.

---

## 3. Verbal identity

- **Tagline (primary):** *Rest is a flex.*
- **Tagline rotation:** *Stay horizontal.* · *Professionally rested.* · *The art of doing less.*
- **Manifesto (put on the About page + a poster):**
  > We don't do hustle. We don't wear tired like a medal.
  > Rest isn't the reward for the work — it *is* the work.
  > Sleep is self-respect. Slowness is a skill. Staying horizontal is a flex.
  > Welcome to the Club.
- **Tone of voice:** calm, dry wit, confident, unbothered, a little luxe. Never loud, never
  salesy, never "BUY NOW 🔥." Think a well-rested friend, not an infomercial.
- **Lexicon (build the world):** members = "the Horizontally Gifted"; the ritual = "the
  Horizontal Hour"; the weekly reset = "Sunday Reset"; the vibe = "expensive calm."

## 4. Visual identity

**Palette — "quiet-luxury sleep" (warm neutrals + one deep night accent):**

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Base light | Cloud Cream | `#F4EEE4` |
| Primary neutral | Warm Oat | `#E4D8C5` |
| Warm accent | Muted Clay | `#C7A78F` |
| Soft green | Sage Mist | `#AEB9A4` |
| Text / dark | Espresso | `#3B2F27` |
| Night accent | Midnight Ink | `#20263A` |

**Typography:** elegant high-contrast **serif** for logo + headlines (editorial/luxury feel;
free options: *Fraunces* or *Cormorant Garamond*), clean humanist **sans** for body
(free: *Inter* or *Geist*).

**Logo:** lowercase serif wordmark with a thin **horizon line** running under/through the
word (a "horizontal" made literal). Espresso on Cloud Cream. Lots of negative space.

**Aesthetic / mood:** quiet-luxury bedroom, warm morning light, rumpled linen, matcha on the
nightstand, film grain, negative space. Everything says *expensive calm*.

## 5. Product line (Printify POD — elevated, not generic)

Use Printify's **premium/heavyweight** blanks (e.g., Comfort Colors / heavy organic cotton)
— the fabric *is* the premium signal. Tonal prints (ink close to the garment colour) read
far more luxe than high-contrast slogans.

| 3 | **"Do Not Disturb" oversized tee** | Minimal DND type, back-print option | Oversized/heavy tee |

**Bundle (raise AOV, sell the identity):** "The Starter Kit — tee + mug + tote" as a
*Member's Kit* at a bundle price.

## 8. Launch sequence (fits your stack + the 3-hour plan)

1. Generate the **logo + palette** (7a) → lock the look.
2. Generate **5 hero designs** (7b) → IP-clean, tonal, on-palette.
3. Generate **mockups** (7c/7d) → pick winners.
4. Push to **Printify** (premium blanks) → set the **premium prices** above.
5. Connect **Shopify + TikTok Shop**; write listings with the **brand voice** (7f).
6. **Vercel** landing page = the manifesto + the Member's Kit + email capture.
7. Post **3 organic ad videos** (7e); build the retargeting audience (75%+ viewers).
8. Order **samples**, QA fabric/print, then scale spend only after a clean offer test.

**Moat maintenance:** stay in-world on every post, do **limited drops**, name your people
("the Horizontally Gifted"), reply in-voice, grow the email/SMS list, feature UGC. The brand
*is* the defensibility.
