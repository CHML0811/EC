# Strategy Correction — the beginner-fit version

Written for: **no ecommerce experience · based in Hong Kong · selling to the US · no product
in hand · doesn't want to make daily content.** The earlier plan quietly assumed the opposite
on all four. This fixes it.

---

## The two problems, and the one idea that solves both

**Problem 1 — photos.** Sourced novelty (shark suit, silhouettes) means you need product
photos you don't have and can't take.

**Problem 2 — channel.** TikTok organic needs you filming American cookouts and lit
suburban windows, daily, from Hong Kong. That was never going to happen.

**The fix: switch the goods to print-on-demand.**

Printify **auto-generates professional product mockups for free.** Upload a design, pick a
t-shirt, and it produces studio photos on models and flat-lays. That is the photo problem
gone entirely — no supplier images, no camera, no samples.

And POD changes everything downstream:

| | Sourced novelty | **Print-on-demand** |
|---|---|---|
| Product photos | You must source them | ✅ **Auto-generated, free** |
| Upfront cost | Sample + shipping | ✅ **$0** |
| Supplier quality risk | High, unseen | ✅ Low — known printers |
| Margin | 30–40% | ✅ **50–70%** |
| Ships from | China → 10–21 days | ✅ **US printer → 3–5 days** |
| Your effort | Sourcing, QC, chasing | ✅ Upload a file |

**US-based printing is the underrated part.** Your buyers get their gift in 3–5 days instead
of three weeks, which is the single biggest cause of refunds and angry comments in gift
selling.

## The channel: Etsy first, not TikTok

This is the bigger correction. Ranked for **your** situation:

| Channel | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Etsy** | ⭐ Best | **Buyers are already searching** "funny gift for uncle." No audience, no content, no ad spend needed. Gag and personalised gifts are one of Etsy's largest categories. |
| **2 · TikTok Shop affiliate** | ⭐ Strong | You list the product; **US creators make the videos** for commission. You never film anything. This is the version of TikTok that fits you. |
| **3 · Pinterest** | Good | Gift shoppers plan there. Image-based, evergreen, near-zero effort — pin the Printify mockups. |
| **4 · Google Shopping / SEO** | Slow build | Passive once running. Product feed from Shopify. Months, not weeks. |
| 5 · TikTok organic | ❌ Skip | Needs you filming daily, in the US, with product in hand |
| 6 · Paid ads | ❌ Not yet | Needs budget + creative + skill. Come back at ~$5k/mo revenue |

**Why Etsy is genuinely the right first move:** every other channel requires you to *create
demand*. Etsy has people typing "funny retirement gift" into a search bar with a credit card
already out. You just have to be the best-looking result. For a beginner with no audience,
that difference is everything.

**Shopify stays** as your brand home and the place Etsy buyers land later. Etsy is where the
first sales come from.

## What success looks like early

Don't expect a viral hit. Expect this:

| When | Realistic |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | Listings live on Etsy. 0–3 sales. Normal. |
| Weeks 3–6 | Etsy learns your listings. 5–20 sales. First reviews. |
| Months 2–3 | 20–60 sales/mo. You learn which products people actually want. |
| Month 4+ | Scale winners, add channels, revisit the sourced products |

Etsy is slow for the first three weeks and then compounds. Most people quit at week two.
