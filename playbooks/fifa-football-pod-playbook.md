# FIFA / Football-Culture POD Playbook (July 2026)

Everything below is grounded in live research pulled on 2026-07-21, two days after
the World Cup final. Sources are listed at the bottom.

---

## 1. What actually happened (the demand context)

- **Spain won the 2026 World Cup**, beating Lionel Messi's Argentina **1–0** after extra
  time (Ferran Torres, 105') at MetLife Stadium on **July 19, 2026** — Spain's 2nd title.
- The tournament is **over**. Tournament-specific hype is now *declining*. Champion
  (Spain) afterglow runs hot for roughly **2–3 weeks**, then fades.
- **The evergreen trend is football-as-fashion**, and it is *rising*: soccer jerseys are
  **+527% on TikTok Shop**. The current #1 soccer product is a **Mexico "Chichén Itzá /
  Aztec" graphic jersey** (6,195 orders, **+1,229% in 7 days**) — a *cultural design*,
  not an official kit. "Blokecore" and "pitch style" are named 2026 fashion trends.
- **Buyers are purchasing cultural identity, not team-accurate replicas.** This is the
  whole opportunity, and it is the legal lane.

## 2. The IP rules — read before every design

The World Cup period is the most aggressive IP-enforcement window of the year. FIFA and
licensing partners actively monitor e-commerce and take listings down fast; penalties
include instant removal, **withheld payouts, and permanent store closure**.

| ❌ BANNED (will get you taken down) | ✅ SAFE (your lane) |
|---|---|
| "FIFA", "World Cup", "World Cup 2026", "WC26" + phonetic look-alikes | National **flags & flag colours** (public domain) |
| Official logo, mascot, trophy, emblem | National symbols: stars & stripes, maple leaf, sun motif, etc. |
| The official FIFA **typeface** | **Cultural / heritage patterns** (Aztec, azulejo, zellige, tartan) |
| Club **crests / badges** & official kit designs | **City names & founding-year** typography |
| Player **names, faces, signatures, numbers-as-identity** | Generic football motifs: ball, pitch, formation, chants (folk) |
| Trademarked terms in **titles, tags, or backend metadata** | Original slogans + a distinct **color story** |

**One-line rule:** *If it identifies a real team, player, or the tournament, don't print
it. If it evokes a country's or city's football culture, you're clear.*

## 4. Validation before you spend on ads

Do this in ~30 minutes per concept (matches your own "product-aware, brand-blind" method):

1. **Google Trends (3-yr view):** compare the *generic* term ("blokecore", "soccer jersey
   outfit", "football culture tee", "[country] soccer aesthetic") vs. the *branded* term.
   You want the generic term rising and no single brand owning it.
2. **TikTok Creative Center → Top Products / hashtags:** confirm the angle is getting
   organic views right now (search "blokecore", "soccer aesthetic", "[country] jersey").
3. **vidIQ:** find "get ready for the match / matchday fit" and "[country] fan" creator
   videos; note the exact fan language they use — reuse it verbatim in your copy.
4. **Printify:** confirm the base product (jersey/heavy tee) has a supplier with good
   print quality + reasonable ship time to your target market.

**Kill rule:** if a concept can't clear a cold add-to-cart ≥ 5% and a CPA at or below your
contribution margin after 2 creative rounds, kill or reposition it — don't automate a loser.

## 5. The 3-hour launch timeline

| Time | Do | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Pick 3 concepts (#1, #2, #3). Run the Trends/TikTok validation. | Claude, Trends, TikTok |
| 0:20–0:30 | **IP clearance pass** on each idea (human, use the table above). | You |
| 0:30–1:10 | Generate 3–5 designs per concept. | Higgsfield / image gen |
| 1:10–1:30 | Generate product mockups + pick winners. | Higgsfield |
| 1:30–2:00 | Push designs to Printify, set products, prices, variants. | Printify |
| 2:00–2:20 | Connect Printify → Shopify **and/or** TikTok Shop; write listings. | Claude for copy |
| 2:20–2:30 | Spin up a Vercel link-in-bio / landing page. | Vercel |
| 2:30–3:00 | Generate 3 ad videos + captions; queue organic posts. | Higgsfield + Claude |

Then: order samples, and only after a clean offer test do you scale spend + automate.

> **Channel note:** the soccer demand is exploding *on TikTok Shop* (+527%). Lead with
> **TikTok Shop + organic creator-style video**; keep Shopify (Printify-connected) as the
> owned store and the retargeting destination. Vercel = a fast landing page for ads.
