# What to sell next — and what not to

The line is 16 certificates at $32–58. The question isn't "what else could we make", it's
**which specific weakness the next product fixes.** There are three, and they're not equal.

| Weakness | Severity | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| **AOV is one item** | 🔴 Critical | Every order is $32. Ad spend can't work at that number. |
| **LTV ≈ AOV** | 🔴 Critical | Someone buys for one retirement and never comes back. |
| **Seasonality** | 🟡 Real | Cohort 2 is a December spike, not a base. |

A new SKU that doesn't fix one of those is just more work.

---

## The market data

- **Posters under 18×24 return 40–55% net margin** on Printify after base cost, shipping,
  platform fees and ad spend. **Mugs return 45–60%.** Both beat apparel decisively —
  which is why the line is paper. ([Printify margins](https://podvector.ai/articles/printify/products/the-complete-guide-to-printifys-most-profitable-products), [POD margins 2026](https://mydesigns.io/blog/print-on-demand-profit-margin/))
- **Drinkware is the third-largest POD category** behind apparel and home decor, with
  year-round demand and peaks at exactly our occasions — Mother's Day, Father's Day,
  Christmas. 11oz base runs **$3.50–8.25**, retail **$18.99–24.99**. ([Printify category data](https://printify.com/blog/most-profitable-print-on-demand-products/), [selling mugs on Etsy](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/selling-mugs-on-etsy))
- **Personalization commands a premium** and is the top-searched gift attribute on Etsy in
  2026, alongside occasion-tied gifting. ([Etsy trends](https://printify.com/blog/top-etsy-searches/), [Etsy niches](https://printify.com/blog/top-selling-niches-on-etsy/))
- **Corporate recognition is a budgeted line item, and humor is explicitly the 2026 shift** —
  companies are moving off generic gift cards toward personalized and playful recognition.
  Bulk pricing is a standard buyer expectation in that market. ([recognition trends](https://cascadebusnews.com/employee-recognition-in-2026/), [funny award demand](https://www.boxupgifting.com/blogs/corporate/funny-employee-award-ideas))

## The three candidates

### 🥇 1. The departmental order — *same product, different buyer*

**Fixes: AOV (critically), seasonality, and LTV.** Costs nothing to launch.

An office buying certificates for a team of ten is a **$250 order instead of $32**. Same
posters, same Printify products, same artwork. No new supply chain, no new design work, no
new provider to vet.

It also fixes seasonality, because offices recognize people year-round — reviews,
onboarding, farewells, work anniversaries — not just in December. And it's the only
candidate that fixes LTV, because a company that does this once does it every year.

The margin is *better* than retail, which is counterintuitive but simple: bulk orders go
through Shopify direct, so Etsy's cut never applies at all. Only payment processing does.

| | Retail via Etsy | Bulk via Shopify |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unit price | $32.00 | $23.00 |
| Print cost | −$10.00 | −$10.00 |
| Platform | −$3.49 (Etsy ~11%) | −$0.97 (payments only) |
| **Kept per unit** | **$18.51** | **$12.03** |
| **Kept per order** | **$18.51** | **$300.75** (×25) |

Less per unit — a wider gap than it first looked, now the fee is right. **Still sixteen
times more per order**, which is the point. Fee maths: [`where-to-sell.md`](where-to-sell.md).

> ⚠️ **One number to verify before publishing the tiers.** Printify charges shipping per
> item with a reduced rate for each additional item — it is *not* one flat parcel fee for
> 25 posters. Price a 25-unit order in Printify's calculator first. If per-item shipping
> is above ~$2, raise the tiers. Don't publish the table until that's checked.

**Status: live on the storefront now** as *Form 7B — Departmental Requisition*.

### 🥈 2. Mugs — *the right second SKU, but not yet*

**Fixes: AOV (as an attach), partially LTV.**

Best margin in the catalog (45–60%), the largest POD category we're not in, and the Bureau
IP transfers with zero rework — "Certified Uncle" is already written. Natural attach:
certificate + mug at checkout takes a $32 order to $54.

Two real risks. Mugs **break in transit**, which means returns and replacements that paper
never generates. And they're heavier, so shipping economics differ from everything we
currently know.

**Do not build this until ten certificates have sold.** Adding SKUs before validating the
first one is how a shop ends up with forty listings and no sales. The certificates are
unproven — every hour on mugs is an hour not spent getting the first order.

### 🥉 3. Greeting cards — *skip*

Cheap to make and genuinely on-brand, but retail lands at $6–8. Even at 50% margin that's
$3–4 an order. The work per listing is identical to a $32 certificate. **Only worth doing
as a free add-on inside a bulk order**, never as a product.

## The sequence

| When | Do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Now** | Departmental tiers live on the storefront | $0 cost, fixes the worst weakness |
| **Now** | Verify Printify's bulk shipping math | The tiers are wrong if per-item shipping is high |
| **After 10 orders** | Add mugs to the 4 best-selling designs only | Let the data pick, not the guess |
| **After 25 orders** | Certificate + mug bundle at checkout | Attach rate is where AOV compounds |
| **Never** | Cards as a standalone product | The economics don't survive the labor |

**The gate on all of it:** none of this matters until the first certificate sells. The
correct next action is still Etsy — account, then cohort 1 published.
