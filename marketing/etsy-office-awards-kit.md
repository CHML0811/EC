## Description

> **Everything you need to run an office awards ceremony. Twenty minutes, start to finish.**
>
> Your company gives awards for revenue and targets. Nobody ever gets one for what they're
> actually known for. This fixes that.
>
> The Bureau of Minor Achievements issues official recognition for things that don't
> deserve any — the Certificate of Approximate Punctuality, Meetings That Were Emails,
> Permanent Audio Only, Refrigerator Archaeology. Properly typeset documents with an
> embossed-look seal and a serial number, that happen to certify something absurd. The joke
> lands because the paperwork refuses to admit there is one.
>
> **WHAT'S INCLUDED**
>
> ✦ **38 award certificates** — enough that nobody in the room gets left out
> ✦ **The Awards Maker** — open one file in your browser, type a name, press print. No
>   account, no software, no subscription. Works with the internet switched off.
> ✦ **The host's script** — what to actually say, word for word, with timings. Read it off
>   the page if you want to.
> ✦ **A nomination ballot** — let the office vote on who gets what
> ✦ **Fold-over name tents** — four per sheet
> ✦ **Three announcement emails** — announce it, remind people, follow up
>
> **WHY THE SCRIPT MATTERS**
>
> Making the certificates was never the hard part. Standing up in front of forty colleagues
> and holding the room is. The script covers the opening, the rhythm for each award, the
> person who didn't show up, the one who wants to make a speech, and the joke that lands
> wrong. Nothing else you can buy does this.
>
> **HOW IT WORKS**
>
> 1. Download instantly after checkout
> 2. Open AwardsMaker.html in any browser
> 3. Paste your team's names, print the lot
>
> Print at home on any printer, or send the PDFs to a print shop. Yours to keep and reuse
> every year — rewrite the awards and the citations to suit your own office.
>
> **A note on the format.** The Awards Maker is a single HTML file — you open it in Chrome, Safari or Edge like a web page. There is nothing to install, no Canva account, no subscription, and it works with the internet switched off. If you can open a browser you can use it. Every certificate is also included as a ready-to-print image if you'd rather skip the maker entirely.
>
> **PLEASE NOTE**
>
> This is a digital download. No physical item is shipped. Because files can't be returned,
> digital purchases aren't refundable — but if anything doesn't open or print properly,
> message me and I'll sort it out the same day.
>
> Made by one person. If you use it, I'd genuinely love to hear how it went.

## Pricing — $14.99, provisional on the live grid

**Corrected twice now, and this one is against real listings rather than category averages.**

Live party-award listings cluster at **$6–$12**: $6 for 47 office holiday awards, $6.50 for
24 funny office awards, $8.25 for 200 Canva awards, $10 for 50 editable Christmas awards. A
$30/150-award listing exists and has almost no reviews. **Awards are not in the $12–$40
business-template band I originally placed them in.**

$14.99 sits above the cluster without being triple it. The kit genuinely carries more — 38
certificates, a host's script, a ballot, name tents, emails and a working editor — but that
only justifies a premium if the listing *shows* it. A flat page-one screenshot at $14.99
loses to $6.99 every time, which is why photo 1 is now printed certificates on a desk.

**This is provisional.** Open the live grid before publishing:

| What you see in the top organic row | Do |
|---|---|
| $5–$8 with 400+ reviews | Drop to **$9.99**. The premium needs a visible reason we don't have yet. |
| $15–$25 Canva bundles | **Hold $14.99**, or go to $18. |

Test against the grid; don't theorise from a blog table.
