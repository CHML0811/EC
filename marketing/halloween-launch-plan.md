## The angle

This product is **not** about scaring people. It's about being *the house on the street people
talk about*. 2026's Halloween decor trend is explicitly **outdoor, lit, social-shareable
displays that turn a home into a neighbourhood attraction** — and a lit window is the cheapest
possible way to buy that status. You're selling **$26 of neighbourhood reputation.**

The built-in demo is unbeatable: **4pm it's a normal house. 7pm someone slows their car.**
That day→night flip is your 3-second hook, and it costs nothing to film.

## The hooks (test in this order)

**1 · The Day/Night Flip** — *lead with this one*
> **0–3s:** static shot of a plain, boring house in daylight. Text: *"my neighbours think I'm normal"*
> **3s:** hard cut to the same window at night, lit, full of silhouettes.
> **Caption:** "4pm: nothing. 7pm: someone slowed their car. 🎃"

**2 · The Alibi**
> **0–3s:** Text: *"'Babe, you're 35, stop scaring the neighbours'"* → *"Me:"*
> **3s:** the lit window reveal, deadpan, no reaction from the poster.
> **Caption:** "A boundary I'm not willing to discuss."

**3 · The Reaction Flip**
> **0–3s:** doorbell-cam or phone footage of a car slowing / kids crossing the street.
> **3s:** cut to the window that caused it.
> **Caption:** "the street has started taking a different route home"

**4 · The Dare** *(share-bait — the duo mechanic)*
> **Caption:** "if you do your front window, I'll do mine 🤞"
> Sells two sets, and can't be answered without tagging someone.

**5 · The Install** *(objection-killer)*
> 10-second speed-run: peel, press, done. Kills the "will it wreck my windows" doubt.
> **Caption:** "no adhesive. no residue. renters, you're clear."

## 💰 Fix the margin before you spend

At **$26** with roughly **$7** landed cost, your contribution margin is only ~**$14** after
fees. That's a brutally tight CPA ceiling for cold traffic.

**Fix it with a bundle** — sell the *house*, not the window:

> **"Haunt The Whole House" — 3 sets, $65** (vs $78 apart)
> *Front window, side window, and the one upstairs that really does the damage.*

That clears the **$60 free-shipping threshold**, roughly **triples AOV**, and pushes your CPA
ceiling to ~$35 — which is where paid actually becomes viable. **Lead with the bundle in ads;
keep the single set as the entry price.**

## 🚫 IP veto

2026's top costume searches are **K-Pop Demon Hunters** and **Wicked** — both licensed. Do
not make, print, or hashtag them. Generic spooky (hands, figures, shadows) is free and clear.
Ride the category, never the character.
