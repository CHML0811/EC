02

The house rules

What keeps this looking like a brand instead of a clearance bin.

Straight face, always

The product is absurd. The photography, spacing, and type never wink. That gap is the entire joke.

One gag per poster

Two jokes cancel each other out. Pick the funnier one and cut the other.

Name a person, not a niche

“Uncles with adult money” outsells “funny gifts for men” every single time.

Readable at thumbnail

If the hook doesn't land at 120px wide with the sound off, it doesn't exist.

Keep a clean cut

Profanity kills paid reach and gets TikTok Shop listings pulled. Ship an edgy organic version and a clean ad version of every winner.

Punch at situations

Aim the joke at a scenario — cookouts, neighbours, in-laws — never at who someone is. Situational humor travels further and stays sellable.

03

Make the posters

Paste into Higgsfield (or any image model). Swap the bracketed parts per product.

A · Deadpan hero (Format 05)

Photorealistic studio product photograph of [PRODUCT], centered on a seamless
[warm grey] backdrop, single soft key light with a crisp falloff shadow,
high-end catalog composition, shot on 85mm, shallow depth of field, deadpan
serious luxury-advertising treatment of an absurd object, generous negative
space at the top third for headline type, 4:5 vertical, 4k, no text, no logos.

B · In-the-wild call-out (Formats 01 / 02)

Candid photorealistic lifestyle photo, [PERSON TYPE] wearing/using [PRODUCT]
at [BACKYARD COOKOUT / LAKE DOCK / FRONT PORCH AT NIGHT], other people
visibly reacting in the background, natural available light, slight phone-camera
imperfection and grain, unposed documentary feel, negative space at the top for
caption text, 9:16 vertical, 4k.

C · Reaction-flip video (Format 06)

Cinematic 9:16 vertical, 15s, handheld. 0-3s: an ordinary [FAMILY COOKOUT]
scene, nothing unusual, natural sound. At exactly 3s: hard cut — someone walks
in wearing [PRODUCT], deadpan, no acknowledgement. 3-12s: real laughter and
double-takes from the group, quick candid cuts. 12-15s: static hero shot of the
product with clean space for a caption. Warm natural light, phone-camera realism.
