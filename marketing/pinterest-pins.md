# Pinterest — 40 pins for cohort 1

Free traffic, and the timing suits us: Pinterest is a **search engine**, pins keep working for
months rather than hours, and **gift shoppers plan early** — people pin Christmas ideas in
September and October. Pins made now are still working in December.

**Cadence:** 3–5 pins/day, spread out. Don't dump 40 at once.
**Rule:** every pin needs its own image. Five different crops of one certificate = five pins.

---

## Boards to create first

| Board | Description (paste into the board) |

Pin each listing to **its specific board first**, then to **Funny Gift Ideas** a few days later.

---

## The 5 image crops per listing

Same artwork, five different pins. Make these in any editor — or just crop the PNG.

| # | Crop | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Full certificate**, 2:3 vertical | Pinterest favours tall. The whole thing, readable. |
| 2 | **Headline close-up** — just the title line | The joke at thumbnail size |
| 3 | **Seal detail** — the crooked laurel | Proves it's well made, not clip art |
| 4 | **In-situ** — the Printify mockup on a wall or desk | Buyers need to picture it hanging |
| 5 | **Text overlay** — artwork behind, "Funny [X] Gift" over it | Catches the scanner who reads nothing else |

## What to expect

Pinterest is **slower than Etsy and cheaper than everything**. Realistically: near-nothing for
3–4 weeks, then a slow trickle that compounds and doesn't decay.

Treat it as a **December play made in August**. The 40 pins go up now, do very little for a
month, and are indexed and circulating by the time gift searches peak.

**Don't judge it before 60 days.** If the pins aren't getting impressions by then, the titles
aren't matching how people search — rewrite those, not the images.
