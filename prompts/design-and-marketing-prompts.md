## C. Viral video ad prompts (Higgsfield video — the 3-second-hook format)

Structure every ad: **0–3s pattern-interrupt (the hook)** → **3s beat-drop reveal of the
design** → **3–12s identity payoff + proof** → **12–15s CTA**.

### C1. Matchday "get the fit" hook
```
Cinematic 9:16 vertical ad, 15 seconds. 0-3s: fast handheld POV of a fan reacting to a
roaring crowd on TV, dim room, tension. At exactly 3s: hard beat-drop cut to a close-up
of a [country] football-culture graphic tee "[DESIGN]" being pulled on, confetti in
[flag colours] bursting. 3-12s: the fan walks into a packed sports bar of fans, slow-mo,
neon, everyone nodding at the fit. 12-15s: freeze on the design, text-safe space for a CTA.
Energetic, film grain, crowd ambience, no on-screen logos.
```

### C2. "POV: your country just made history" hook
```
Vertical 9:16, 12s. 0-3s: black screen, muffled commentary building, then a sudden roar.
At 3s: quick-cut montage of a fan celebrating in a [flag-colour] graphic tee "[DESIGN]",
scarves in the air, street celebration energy, saturated color. End on a clean product
close-up. Original artwork only, no real crest, no player face.
```

### C3. Blokecore fashion "fit check" hook
```
Vertical 9:16, 12s, retro film look. 0-3s: fast outfit-rotation "fit check" of a person
layering a distressed [blokecore] football-culture tee "[DESIGN]" over jeans, 90s room.
3s: beat-drop freeze on the graphic. Cool color grade, VHS grain, streetwear-catalog
energy, no trademarks.
```

## E. Retargeting setup (the "watched 95% / 75%" audience)

Platforms differ, so target the **heaviest viewers**, not a magic single number:

- **TikTok Ads:** create an **Engagement audience** from video views at **25 / 50 / 75 / 100%**
  thresholds → retarget the 75%+ segment with C-series testimonial cuts + a bundle offer.
- **Meta:** custom audience from "watched 75%/95% of video" + ATC-no-purchase.
- **YouTube/Google:** audience segment from people who viewed the video / engaged with the
  channel.

**Sequence:** cold creator-style hook video → retarget 75%+ viewers with proof + offer →
retarget add-to-cart-no-purchase with urgency. Kill any creative whose comments are
"looks flimsy" / "I can get this cheaper" if CPA stays above margin after 2 rounds.
